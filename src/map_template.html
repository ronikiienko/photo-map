Map of Simple Wikipedia

How to read this

Each dot is one article. Articles about similar things sit close together; darker areas hold many articles. Labels name each region after its most typical articles.

Scroll to zoom, drag to move, hover for titles. Click a dot (or search) to see its 10 most similar articles: selected, neighbours.

Distances on the map are approximate (768 numbers squeezed into 2). The neighbour list is closer to the truth: it is computed from the 50 most important directions of the 768 (PCA), not from the 2D picture.
